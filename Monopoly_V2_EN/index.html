📝 Data Entry Guide

1. Format: Paste 3~5 columns from Excel:
[Type] | [Title] | [Desc] | [Tier (Opt)] | [Tags (Opt)]

2. Logic: Type quest to map to map nodes. Other types (e.g., theory, experiment) will generate Insight Cards automatically.

3. Distribution: Tag Tier as advanced to send cards to the "Literature Review". Leave blank or use base to distribute evenly at start.

4. Validation: Tags set in column 5 require students to submit cards with matching tags to complete the proposal.

📊 Session Recommendations

The board contains 11 Project Nodes. Recommended setup:

  • Groups: 4 teams to balance map space and competitive tension.
  • Quests: Prepare at least 11 quests to fill all nodes.
  • Base Cards: 30~40 cards. Ensures 6~8 cards per team initially (these cards should support students' answers for the quests).
  • Advanced Cards: 10~15 cards in the "Literature Review" to encourage strategic funding use.
